About TheElephantWire

TheElephantWire is an educational financial media project powered by EDGAR — The Transparency Elephant — a patriotic pachyderm with a simple belief: public financial data should actually be public.

Every trading day, EDGAR organizes SEC Form 4 insider trading disclosures and publishes them for free because the information belongs to everyone, not just hedge funds and Bloomberg terminals.

EDGAR loves entrepreneurs, builders, free markets, and people willing to risk their own money on their own ideas. He hates communism, central planning, and economic systems that promise equality and deliver bread lines.

When he is not reading filings, EDGAR takes selfies with economists, founders, patriots, and historical figures in the least likely places imaginable.

All content is provided for informational and educational purposes only and is not investment advice.
